# Support Onboarding Program

**A 30-60-90 day phase-gated onboarding framework for B2B SaaS support teams.**

Built by [Miguel Cruz](https://mpascualcruz.com) · Training & Enablement · 2026

---

## What This Is

A complete support onboarding system designed around three core principles:

1. **Sequence the learning** — product knowledge before ticket handling, ticket mechanics before live queue access, tools before judgment calls
2. **Gate progression on evidence** — new hires advance because they demonstrated readiness, not because time passed
3. **Keep leadership in the loop** — daily trainer logs and structured checkpoints surface risk early, not at Day 75

The program covers Days 1–90 across three phases: Foundation, Application, and Optimization. It includes a day-by-day Week 1 schedule, team lead checkpoint guides at Day 15/30/60, a Trainee Progress Tracker database, a Daily Trainer Log database, a tools access checklist, and process walkthrough references.

## Program Structure

```
Phase 1 — Foundation (Days 1–30)
├── Week 1: Day-by-day time-blocked schedule
├── Weeks 2–4: Deepening, Applying, Reinforcing
├── Day 15 Checkpoint (Team Lead)
└── Day 30 Checkpoint (Team Lead)

Phase 2 — Application (Days 31–60)
├── Independent queue with structured feedback cadence
├── QA rubric introduction (Week 5)
├── Day 45 Self-Assessment
└── Day 60 Checkpoint (Team Lead)

Phase 3 — Optimization (Days 61–90)
├── Edge case handling
├── Escalation judgment
├── Efficiency benchmarks
└── Day 90 Certification Review
```

## Design Decisions

**Why Notion over Google Sheets:**
Notion supports subpages, embedded databases, Notion AI for content expansion, public sharing with one-click duplication, and a searchable interface that new hires can actually navigate. Google Sheets is better for the trainer's daily log export and leadership reporting — both of which are referenced in the "What Comes Next" section of the workspace.

**Why phase gates instead of a fixed timeline:**
A new hire should advance because they demonstrated readiness, not because 30 days passed. Each checkpoint has defined evaluation criteria and documented decision options (Advance / Extend / Risk Flag). This prevents the most common onboarding failure: graduating someone who isn't ready because no one wanted an uncomfortable conversation.

**Why AM heavy / PM hands-on:**
New hires cannot retain eight hours of instruction. Heavy cognitive content (product, policy, tools) in the AM. Interactive, practice-based, and social content in the PM. This is a human factors decision, not a scheduling preference.
