Support Onboarding Program

Category Training & Enablement
Tools Used Notion, Scribe, Loom, Canva, Claude
Format 30-60-90 Day Program
Year 2026
Context

Most support onboarding programs fail in the same way — they front-load policy and product information before a new hire has any context for why it matters, then drop them into the queue with minimal structure. The result is a slow ramp, inconsistent early ticket quality, and a new agent who learns the job by making avoidable mistakes in front of real customers.

A well-sequenced onboarding program doesn't just cover what to do — it explains the operational logic behind it, introduces tools in order of priority, and gives team leads checkpoints to course-correct before bad habits form.

Approach

Designed a 30-60-90 day onboarding program structured around three phases: Foundation (days 1–30, focused on product knowledge, ticket handling mechanics, and shadowing), Application (days 31–60, focused on independent queue work with structured feedback loops), and Optimization (days 61–90, focused on edge cases, escalation judgment, and efficiency benchmarks).

Built the program architecture in Notion with role-specific pages, a daily task structure for week one, process walkthrough references for core workflows, and a team lead checkpoint guide at day 15, 30, and 60. A short Loom walkthrough orients new hires on how to navigate the workspace.

Phase 1 · Days 1–30
Foundation
Product · Tools · Mechanics · Shadowing
  • Product knowledge & tool orientation
  • Ticket handling mechanics
  • Shadowing senior reps
  • First ticket checklist
◇ Day 15 + Day 30 checkpoint
Phase 2 · Days 31–60
Application
Queue · Feedback · QA Introduction
  • Independent queue work
  • Structured feedback cadence
  • Self-assessment at Day 45
  • QA rubric introduced
◇ Day 45 self-assessment + Day 60 checkpoint
Phase 3 · Days 61–90
Optimization
Edge Cases · Judgment · Efficiency
  • Edge case handling
  • Escalation judgment
  • Efficiency benchmarks
  • Certification review
◇ Day 60 go/no-go + Day 90 certification
Outcome

The structured program gave new hires a sequenced learning path instead of a document dump. Team leads had defined checkpoints with evaluation criteria rather than informal check-ins. The process walkthrough references reduced reliance on shadowing for repetitive procedural tasks, and the Notion structure meant the program could be updated without rebuilding it from scratch.

New hires consistently reported higher confidence at the 30-day mark compared to unstructured onboarding. The public template has been made available for any support team to duplicate and adapt.
